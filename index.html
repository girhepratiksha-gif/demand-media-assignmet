Three content initiatives

Three ideas I can start with

Each one is simple to produce, easy to repeat and built to bring executives and readers together.

A. The podcast series

Pulse Room

A 15–20 minute video podcast with an industry leader.

  • A short, engaging introduction
  • A fireside chat on one subject
  • One strong closing takeaway

Builds executive visibility, creates reusable content and opens doors to future partnerships.

B. The quick take

Pulse Rapid Fire

A standalone short video. Five quick questions, spontaneous and easy to share.

Overrated trend?Tech worth backing?Common misconception?Bold prediction?One piece of advice?

Published on its own, not buried inside the podcast.

C. Keep the conversation going

The Pulse Loop

Readers send in questions or business challenges. Selected ones go to an upcoming expert, and the answer comes back as a short video, an article or a social post.

Gives readers a reason to return and executives a reason to take part, with relevant opt-in enquiries.
